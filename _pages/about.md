I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin-Madison in August 2026, after completing an M.S. there in May 2022. I was co-advised by Professors [Mikko Lipasti](https://pharm.ece.wisc.edu/mikko/) and [Joshua San Miguel](https://jsm.ece.wisc.edu/), actively contributing to both of their research groups: [PHARM](https://pharm.ece.wisc.edu/) and [STACS](https://jsm.ece.wisc.edu/). Previously, I earned both my B.S. and M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University in May 2020.

I have broad interests, including: neuromorphic computing, brain-inspired chip design, computer architecture, heterogenous systems and GPU acceleration, neuroscience, neural basis for cognition, speech processing, and sound source localization. I also interested in the cognitive basis for speech processing in the ascending auditory pathway and developing energy efficient spiking neural networks models to replicate/explain this behavior.

Currently, I'm employed as a Silicon Engineer on the TPU team at Google.
