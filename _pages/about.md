I am a computer architecture researcher. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/) in August 2026, after completing an M.S. there in May 2022. Previously, I earned both my B.S. and M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.ece.cmu.edu/) in May 2020.

My work explores computer architecture and specialized hardware, including stochastic computing and neuromorphic speech processing. See my [publications](/publications/), [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HJH7u3kAAAAJ&hl=en), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ranganath-rao-selagamsetty/).
